ABOUT URIM & THUMMIM

Who We Are

URIM & THUMMIM Enterprises is a Kenya-based, multi-sector enterprise focused on business development, project coordination, commercial solutions, technology and inclusive economic growth.

We work to connect businesses, suppliers, producers, institutions, development partners and communities through practical projects and collaborative approaches.
