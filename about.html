OUR STORY

Connecting People, Enterprise & Opportunity

URIM & THUMMIM Enterprises is a Kenya-based, multi-sector enterprise focused on business development, project coordination, commercial solutions, technology and inclusive economic growth.

We bring people and ideas together to help turn opportunity into practical action. By connecting businesses, producers, suppliers, institutions, development partners and communities, we help build initiatives that respond to real needs and create shared value.

Our work spans logistics and trade, agriculture, infrastructure, clean energy, technology and community development. Across every sector, we aim to make collaboration more effective and growth more inclusive.
